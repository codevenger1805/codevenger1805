# 👋 Hi, I'm Komal

### 🎯 Product thinker. Professional overthinker. Occasional developer.

I like taking messy problems, asking **“why is this even happening?”**, and turning them into products people can actually use.

Currently exploring **Product Management, Product Strategy, User Research, Product Analytics, and Product Design** — basically everything between *“we should build this”* and *“okay, but should we?”*

### 🧠 What I'm Into

* 🧩 **Product Strategy** — turning ideas into something that makes sense
* 🔎 **User Research** — figuring out what people actually need vs. what they say they need
* 🗺️ **Roadmapping** — making ambitious ideas slightly less chaotic
* 📊 **Product Analytics** — because opinions are useful, data is useful-er
* 🎨 **Product Design** — creating experiences that don't require a tutorial
* 🤝 **Cross-functional Work** — translating between users, designers, developers, and business people

### 🚧 Currently Building

* 🚀 Product concepts and real-world projects
* 🧪 Case studies around product thinking and problem solving
* 📈 Learning analytics, experimentation, and growth
* 💡 Turning random ideas into things that occasionally become actual projects

### 🎤 Outside the Product Bubble

When I'm not obsessing over products:

* 🎙️ Public speaking & storytelling
* 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Community building
* 🎪 Event management
* 🧭 Leadership & team coordination

### 💭 My Product Philosophy

> **Don't build more features. Build something people actually need.**

Or, more realistically:

> **“Wait… who is this feature actually for?”**
